Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't

📅

Diary View

A beautiful calendar view of everything you've logged. Tap any day to see what you consumed that day.

📋

Ranked Lists

Build public or private ranked and unranked lists. Your top albums of the decade. Best films of 2024. All yours.

🔖

Watchlist

Save anything to your watchlist and it stays neatly tucked away — separate from your diary until you actually log it.

🌍

Discover

See what's trending on Press Play. Browse popular films, albums, books and games being logged by the community.

📊

Stats

Monthly recaps, year in review, genre breakdowns and average ratings. Understand your taste at a glance.

🔔

Notifications

Get notified when someone likes your review, comments on your log, or follows you. Stay in the loop.

👥

Follow Friends

See a real-time feed of everything your friends are logging. React, comment, and discover what they love.
